How to Play

  • 📱
    Tilt your phone left and right to steer Boo through the maze.
  • 👆
    Hold anywhere on the game to make Boo float upward. Release to drift downward.
  • Reach the glowing jack-o'-lantern portal to finish the maze.
  • 🍬
    Candy restores moves, potions add time, and lollipops restore both. Some are hidden down misleading side paths.
  • 🦇
    Creatures outside the paths are decorations and cannot hurt Boo.
